## What ran, and on what

Two new device profiles were built **from the system image already installed**
(`android-36;google_apis;x86_64`), so nothing was downloaded:

| Profile | AVD | Screen | Density | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phone (the usual one) | `SubRadar_API_36` | 1080×2400 | 440 dpi | Everything in this plan: U1–U4, U6, U7, and the 38 visual baselines |
| **Small phone** (U5.4) | `Zeno_Small` (Nexus S) | **480×800** | 240 dpi | Boots, installs, runs. **F240** found here |
| **Tablet** (U5.4) | `Zeno_Tablet` (Medium Tablet) | **2560×1600** landscape | 320 dpi | Boots, installs, runs. Nothing clipped: the full sample ledger, the committed total and the caveat all render |

All three are API 36. The two new AVDs were booted on their own ports (5556, 5558) so the
5554 emulator another session may be using was left alone, and both were shut down after.

## F240 · the sample label falls below the fold on a small phone

Onboarding's first beat shows a sample ledger — Netflix $15.99, Spotify $10.99, ChatGPT
Plus $20.00, iCloud+ $2.99, Figma $12.00 — then a committed total of $61.97/mo, then the
line that makes it honest: **"Sample figures — your ledger starts empty."**

On the 480×800 phone the accessibility tree shows the last three of those collapsed:

```
'Figma'                                      [36,599][93,616]
'Committed'                                  [36,659][143,616]   <- top below bottom
'$61.97 /mo'                                 [305,656][444,616]
'Sample figures — your ledger starts empty.' [36,715][444,616]
```

A swipe brings them back (`'Sample figures …' [36,555][444,598]`, height 43), so **nothing
is unreachable** — it is below the fold, not lost. But the screen's obvious action is the
Continue button, the scroll indicator is hidden, and nothing signals there is more. A
person on a small phone can reasonably take five named services at real prices for real
data, which is exactly what that line exists to prevent.

Low severity, and a judgement call about a hero screen rather than a bug with one right
answer, so it is **D23 for the owner**:

- **(a)** Move the caveat above the sample rows, so the label always precedes the data it
  qualifies, on every screen size. Smallest change, and better order to read in.
- **(b)** Show fewer sample rows on a short viewport, so the block always fits.
- **(c)** Accept it: the data is plainly illustrative and the next beat explains the app.

**Recommendation: (a).**

## U5.7 and U5.8 · the website in Firefox and WebKit

Done in W7 and running in CI, not re-done here: `apps/web/playwright.config.ts` has
`firefox` and `webkit` projects alongside Chrome, installed in CI and run one engine after
another with two workers. WebKit goes through a self-signed TLS front
(`e2e/tls-proxy.mjs`) because it applies `upgrade-insecure-requests` even to 127.0.0.1.
Firefox's wording of the policy message, its tab stop on a scrollable region and its focus
staying on the last control are all allowed for, and nav links are held to 24 px in every
engine (axe target-size, which WebKit caught).

## On the visual baselines for these profiles (U2.3, U2.4)

Both profiles now render and were inspected, which is what found F240. Baseline **sets**
were not captured and committed for them: the committed phone set is already 4.4 MB of
binaries that churn on every deliberate UI change, and these two AVDs are ad hoc rather
than part of CI. `scripts/app-visual.mjs` takes any device through `ANDROID_SERIAL`, so a
set can be produced for either profile when there is somewhere to run it — which is the
same open question as U2.7.
