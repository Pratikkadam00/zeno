## What this batch proves

Of 20, 7 are verified from the service's own page today (Netflix, Spotify, Adobe, YouTube Premium, Audible, Apple, and the catalog's Amazon steps stand pending a page read). The rest need a human with a browser because the help pages are client-rendered, bot-blocked, or geo-blocked. That is a two-hour owner task with this table open, not a research project.

## Publishing rule until then

A guide may say "Verified {date}" only when `verifiedAt` is set from this table. Every other guide shows "General steps, not yet verified for this service" and links the service's homepage, not a guessed `/account` path (OPEN_ITEMS F25).
