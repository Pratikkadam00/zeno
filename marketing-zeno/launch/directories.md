## Shared copy

**Short description (≤120 chars, 113 used):** Subscription tracker that finds, tracks and cancels subscriptions from receipts and statements. No bank login required.

**Full description:**

Zeno is a subscription tracker and budgeting app for people who refuse to hand a bank login to an app. It discovers subscriptions from email receipts you scan or a bank statement CSV you export yourself, warns you 7 days, 3 days and the morning before every renewal, flags free trials before they convert, and gives you a step-by-step cancellation guide for each service. A cancellation stays pending until the next renewal passes without a charge. Data is stored encrypted on your device; an account is optional. Free for up to 10 subscriptions. Pro is $3.99/month or $29.99/year. Lifetime is $79.99 once.
